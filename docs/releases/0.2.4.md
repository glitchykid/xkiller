Xkiller 0.2.4 makes the research workspace fit a compact desktop window without scrolling.

- Fifteen internal views separate market analysis, training settings and quality, simulation results and history, journal execution and costs, data tools and methodology.
- Dense 30 px controls, 8 px gaps and charts that use the available height retain the dark Gunmetal palette and generated minimalist icon.
- Sixteen generated transparent PNG glyphs replace vector icons. Their edges and chart geometry are antialiased; the application icon retains its fully opaque Gunmetal background.
- Adaptive table pages replace scrolling. Filtering and resizing clamp the page, background polling preserves it, and CSV export remains complete.
- Form values survive view changes. Completed operations open their result view. Tabs support Arrow/Home/End navigation and accessible labels.
- The default window is 1180 × 760; the minimum is 1024 × 720. All six languages were verified down to a 1024 × 660 content area.
- English documentation and screenshots are updated, including the TDD workflow and boundary checklist.

All 28 automated checks pass, including instant job completion, raster transparency/opacity and release metadata checks. The 15 views were checked in all six languages at three desktop sizes, with additional empty-state and running-job fixtures.

Download **Xkiller-0.2.4-x64.exe** for Windows x64. Electron and the .NET runtime are included. **SHA256SUMS.txt** contains the checksum. The executable is unsigned.

This release updates presentation and navigation in the Bybit ETHUSDT learning and historical simulation lab. It does not change research calculations or submit real orders.
